## Post 2: The Demo Tease (pair with screen recording or screenshots)

Eto yung ginawa namin.

Hindi pa siya perfect pero it works. And we ship updates every week based on what users tell us.

**loona.ph** — check it out, tell us what you think.

## Post 3: Behind the Build

Real talk — we didn't build Loona for banks or corporations.

We built it for yung lender na nag-ttrack sa notebook. Yung may 10-20 borrowers na kinocompute pa sa utak yung balances. Yung walang budget for expensive software.

That's who this is for. Libre siya. Sa browser lang.

If that's you or someone you know — **loona.ph**
